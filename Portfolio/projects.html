Key Features

01

AI-Powered Character

A little animated 3D character that cheers you on when you answer math questions correctly — all made from AI using Poly Pizza assets.

02

Music-Driven Learning

Play along with the game and answer math problems while the music is playing. Each correct answer restores a missing instrument track.

03

5 Progressive Levels

Pick what level of math you want to play — levels 1 through 5, each with different songs, beats, and increasingly challenging equations.

04

Chaotic Audio Feedback

Wrong answers create distorted, chaotic audio effects — making you WANT to find the right answer to fix the music.

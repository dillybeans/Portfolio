About Me

My Story

Hello, I am Dylan Dodge and I am a 7th grader at Veritas Classical Academy in Phoenix, Arizona. I have been taking classes on coding for about 6 years now and I find it very enjoyable — what you can create is limitless.

I have always been a creative person and have loved making things, but I never knew how to bring my ideas to life until I discovered coding. So many people struggle with math and it makes it hard to have fun with it. But I wanted to make it fun and educational at the same time.

When I was younger I would sing and listen to music all the time (I still do) and I realized that math and music had a lot in common — rhythms, beats, fractions — so I wanted to make a game that would help people learn math while also having fun.
